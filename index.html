Home and product page redesign

Four design directions for the full journey: the company home page, the Digital Products hub, the WayFinder Horizon storefront and all four therapeutic area profiles (Obesity, NSCLC, Heart Failure and Rheumatoid Arthritis). All four use the same content, taken from the current site and the WayFinder files.
